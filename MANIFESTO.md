# The Dream Engineering Manifesto

**Scientific dream engineering research.**

Every night, the brain builds worlds no one designed. Vivid, emotional, impossible — and gone by morning.
We think those worlds can be engineered. Not by mystics, not by marketers, but by method.

## What we believe

**1. Dreams are engineerable.**
The sleeping brain still listens. A single spoken line at the edge of sleep can carry a theme into a dream. This is not a hope; it has been measured. We start from what is measured.

**2. Measure everything.**
A dream we can't measure is a story. Every session is randomised. Every report is scored blind. Controls are mandatory. A cue that "feels like it worked" proves nothing until the data agrees.

**3. Say what is known, and what is not.**
Findings are findings. Hypotheses are labelled as hypotheses. Null results get published with the same weight as wins. When we don't know, we say so.

**4. Your mind, your intention.**
Dream engineering is something you do *to yourself*, *for yourself*. You choose the theme. You can stop at any time. It never optimises for engagement, streaks or anyone else's interests.

**5. Your dreams never leave your device.**
No accounts. No cloud. No data harvesting. The most private thing you have stays yours.

**6. Safety before ambition.**
Positive themes only. Sleep comes first. Check-ins pause the work automatically if anything feels wrong. This is research on ourselves, and we treat ourselves with care. It is not medicine, and it doesn't pretend to be.

**7. Open, so it can be proven wrong.**
Every line of code, every protocol, every analysis is open source. Replicate it. Challenge it. Break it. Science that can't be checked isn't science.

**8. Wonder is the point.**
Rigour is how we get there, not why we go. We do this because the dreaming mind is the most beautiful unexplored territory we carry with us every night.

## The question we are chasing

Does emotion carry meaning into dreams?
If a feeling can slip through the gate of sleep and pull an idea in with it, then dreams become something we can work *with* — not just remember.

We intend to find out. Honestly.

*Sleep well — and measure everything.*
**The Dream Engineering Project**
